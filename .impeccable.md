## Design Context

### Users
People looking for clothes they have seen in a photo, across Amazon India and Meesho. The brief comes from the creator's image-to-clothing shopping request. They need product photos, relevant details, real shop links, and understandable evidence.

### Brand Personality
Tactile, direct, discerning. Fashion shopping studio was the proposed direction in an optional question; no answer was available when work proceeded, so this tone is an explicit design assumption.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, warm neutral product workspace. Condensed garment-label typography paired with legible public-service sans. Rust actions. Product photos carry the visual weight; no marketing hero, fake browser chrome, or invented lifestyle photography.

### Design Principles
- Bring the shortlist beside the reference, rather than below a large browser demo.
- Make evidence visible without mistaking checked links for exact visual matches.
- Keep the main action obvious; expose browser decisions through progressive disclosure.
- Keep native inputs, focus rings, readable contrast, and single-column phone layouts.

Font exploration: rejected reflex choices Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif and DM Sans. Selected Barlow Condensed and Public Sans after checking their Google Fonts specimen pages: garment-label display with readable UI copy.
